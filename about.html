About Us

Smart City Management System

The Smart City Management System is a web-based platform developed to provide organized and accessible information about essential facilities available in selected areas.

Our project focuses on Vashi, Kharghar, Belapur and Panvel, allowing users to explore information about important services through a simple and user-friendly interface.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this system is to bring essential city information together in one organized platform, making it easier for users to explore available facilities in their area.

Our Objective

Our objective is to develop a structured digital platform that presents city information clearly, improves accessibility, and provides a convenient way for users to explore essential facilities.
